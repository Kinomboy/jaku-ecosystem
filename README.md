JAKU Ecosystem

An independent project in artificial intelligence, software development, and computational research.

Status: Under construction

My name is Jeisson Andres Polo Motta, and I’m the creator of JAKU.

I started JAKU with the idea of building an AI workspace where I could work with different models, develop software, organize projects, and have more control over how tasks are carried out.

As development progressed, I realized that JAKU could become more than one application. That is where the idea of JAKU Ecosystem comes from.

The goal is to bring different tools and areas of work together, while allowing each one to develop for its own purpose.

What I’m building

JAKU currently has several areas of development:

* JAKU Core: The foundation of the system, including models, tools, workflows, and operational controls.
* JAKU Code: A workspace focused on programming, software development, testing, and debugging.
* JAKU BIO-LAB MAX: An independent computational research project exploring scientific literature, public datasets, and reproducible analysis.

I also want to develop shared research methods that help JAKU evaluate evidence, identify mistakes, and distinguish between a promising idea and a result that has actually been demonstrated.

Current progress

JAKU is still in development.

Most of the work is taking place privately, and there is no public software release available yet.

I’m using this repository to introduce the ecosystem, document its purpose, and gradually share information about its development.

For now, this repository contains documentation only.

Why I’m sharing it

I believe useful technology and scientific knowledge should be open to discussion, criticism, and improvement.

My long-term goal is to make JAKU useful not just for my own work, but also for people interested in building software, learning, and carrying out careful research—with or without AI.

There is still a lot to build, test, and improve. I prefer to share the project as it actually stands rather than present unfinished work as a completed product.
